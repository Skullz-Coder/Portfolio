⚠ Never share personal details on random websites

You just typed your name into a form on a website you don't know. Fake websites collect exactly this kind of information to scam or track people.

This page doesn't save or send anything you type. Before you enter personal details anywhere, check these three things:

  • Does the web address look right and start with https://?
  • Do you know and trust who runs the site?
  • Does the site really need this information?
